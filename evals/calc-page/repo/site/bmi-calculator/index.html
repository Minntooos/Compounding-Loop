BMI calculator

Body mass index, usually shortened to BMI, compares your weight with your height. Enter your weight in kilograms and your height in metres, then press the button to see the result. The calculation divides your weight by the square of your height. For example, a person who weighs seventy kilograms and is one point seven five metres tall has a BMI of about twenty two point nine. BMI is only a screening number and says nothing about muscle, bone or where body fat sits, so talk to a health professional before acting on it. The figures here follow the World Health Organization ranges for adults. Children use different charts. If you enter zero, a negative number or text, the page tells you what to fix instead of showing a broken value.
